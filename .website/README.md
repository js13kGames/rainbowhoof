Rainbowhoof — a tiny unicorn gallops on its own; drag to paint rainbows and gallop across the dusk hills on them. Dive to bank momentum, launch off crests, chain mid-air catches to grow your multiplier, and scoop stardust to refill your ink. 13KB, zero assets: every pixel is drawn and every sound is synthesized in code.
